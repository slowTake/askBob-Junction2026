# Final-cut checklist

The review step (Gemini) and a human both go through this before upload.

- [ ] `make check` passes: at most 115 s, **title cards included** (Junction's hard limit is 120 s)
- [ ] The answer and its highlight are visible within the first 8 s
- [ ] All four Stoa judging criteria are visible: accuracy, verifiable reasoning, creativity, low inference cost
- [ ] Every product shot is a real recording of the real app; nothing generated or mocked
- [ ] The low-confidence case (phone photo) is shown honestly
- [ ] Models are named and the eval result is shown (X of Y example questions)
- [ ] Aiven is visible in the live answer moment and in the architecture shot
- [ ] Captions are readable on a laptop screen; UI text is large enough
- [ ] No background music, no AI-generated b-roll
- [ ] Uploaded public or unlisted; the link works logged out; the platform shows at most 2:00
